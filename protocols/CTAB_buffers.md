# Preparation of buffers and reagents for CTAB DNA extraction

### CTAB buffer (CTAB 5% (w/v), 0.35M NaCl, 120mM potassium phosphate buffer)

#### Products:

- Hexadecyltrimethylammonium bromide (364.36 g/mol, Merck)
    - synonyms: CTAB, Cetrimonium bromide, Cetyltrimethylammonium bromide, Palmityltrimethylammonium bromide
    - CAS Number: 57-09-0
- K2HPO4 (174.18 g/mol, Merck)
- NaCl (58.44 g/mol, Merck)
- DEPC (Diethyl pyrocarbonate, 162.14 g/mol (store at 4°C), Merck)

#### Method:

- 200 ml distilled water
- 4.2 g K2HPO4
- 4.09 g NaCl
- Adjust the pH to pH = 8 with NaOH
- Add stir bar and autoclave
    - Heat the solution in the microwave If precipitation is observed after autoclaving
- Add 5 µl DEPC
- Heat to 60°C and incubate overnight at 60°C with continuous stirring to remove the DEPC
- Add 10 g CTAB (Hexadecyltrimethylammonium bromide) and heat to 60°C to dissolve the CTAB

### PEG-6000 precipitation solution (polyethylene glycol 30% (w/v), 1.6M NaCl)

- 250 ml distilled water
- 75 g PEG-6000 (Sigma-Aldrich)
- 23.38 g NaCl
- Add stir bar and autoclave
- Add 12.5 µl DEPC
- Stir intense for 20 minutes
- Incubate overnight at 60°C to remove the DEPC
